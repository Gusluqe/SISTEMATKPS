# Proteger Salud — Sistema de Tickets de Soporte Técnico

## Product Purpose
Internal IT support ticket system for Proteger Salud, a health-sector company. Staff report technical problems; the IT team receives, assigns, tracks, and resolves them. Not a public SaaS product — an internal operational tool used daily by non-technical employees and a small IT team.

## Register
product

## Users
- **Reporters (all staff):** Administrative, accounting, HR, sales, operations employees. Non-technical. They submit tickets via the public landing page. They need a form that feels safe and clear, not intimidating. Spanish-speaking.
- **Admins / IT technicians:** 3–5 people. They use the admin panel daily to manage ticket queues, assign work, and update statuses. They need density and clarity over decoration.

## Brand
- Company: Proteger Salud ("protect health") — healthcare sector
- Tone: professional, calm, trustworthy. Not cold. Not marketing-heavy.
- Language: Spanish (Argentina, vos/ustedes form)
- No public brand guidelines — internal tool, functional identity

## Anti-references
- No generic SaaS dashboards with hero metrics and rainbow gradients
- No hospital-white clinical look
- No neon-aggressive fintech energy
- No startup playfulness (this is a serious operational tool)

## Design principles
1. Clarity first — every element must earn its place
2. Dense but not cluttered — admin panel needs information density
3. Status must be scannable at a glance — tickets need instant visual priority parsing
4. Form trust — the public form must feel safe and competent to non-technical users
5. Dark environment — IT ops tools are used on monitors in office environments, sometimes dim
